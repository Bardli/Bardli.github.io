I build intelligent systems that push medicine forward. As an ML Engineer at the UHN AI Hub, I develop computer vision and NLP solutions for clinical research — from automated 3D vertebrae segmentation achieving 0.96 Dice scores across 73 CT scans, to RAG-powered knowledge systems for pharmaceutical compliance. My C/C++ code is currently orbiting Earth aboard the York University CubeSat.

Previously, I designed agentic AI frameworks at XtalPi, led data pipelines at TechUnited.ngo, and conducted deep learning security research at York University. I hold a Master of Engineering in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Toronto and a First-Class Honours BSc in Computer Science from York University.
